I am **Chava Berjan**.

My career in technology has spanned infrastructure, networking, services, cloud, automation and cybersecurity. Along the way, I have learned to see technology not as isolated disciplines but as capabilities that must work together in day-to-day operations.

I want to understand what problem a tool solves, how it integrates with other systems, what operating it involves, how to assess its results and how to turn experience into reusable knowledge.

My current work focuses on **cybersecurity**, while I continue to explore infrastructure, operations, workplace culture, automation and artificial intelligence.

## This website

**chava.berjan.mx** brings together articles, experiences and knowledge I have shared over the years.

In [Articles](/en/articles), I develop ideas and reflections. The [Archive](/en/archive) preserves references to earlier pieces published elsewhere, with their original context.

In [Professional memories](/en/experience/memorias-profesionales), I share photographs and memories of different stages of my career, colleagues and experiences. These include [Fortinet Xtreme Team](/en/experience/fortinet-xtreme-team), its technical training events, and [Technology events](/en/experience/encuentros-tecnologicos) with Google and AWS, where I have had the opportunity to learn and exchange experiences.

I also share technical work on [GitHub](https://github.com/gersonberjan).

## Training and certifications

My background includes professional certifications and courses across several disciplines. You can find my public badges on [Credly](https://www.credly.com/users/salvador-gerson-berjan-rosas).

Rather than simply listing credentials, I want to share how learning becomes experience and leads to better decisions.
