# Explaining an Information Security Management System

<div className="memories-mobile-back"><a href="/en/experience/memorias-profesionales">← Back to Memories</a></div>

Throughout my career at CONTPAQi, I have had opportunities to share knowledge and experience in different settings. On this occasion, together with my leader Pepe, I took part in a series of interviews explaining what an Information Security Management System (ISMS) is.

We shared our perspectives on how it works, the benefits of establishing processes and policies, and the importance of making security part of an organization's everyday activities.

One thing I appreciate when revisiting these videos is noticing that some aspects of how I explain things have stayed with me. One is the effort to make complex ideas easier to understand by connecting them to simpler, more familiar situations through analogies.
