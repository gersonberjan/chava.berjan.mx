# Fortinet Xtreme Team

<div className="memories-mobile-back"><a href="/en/experience/memorias-profesionales">← Back to Memories</a></div>

*The Fortinet technical community.*

Between 2007 and 2014, I had the opportunity to participate in **Fortinet Xtreme Team**, advanced technical training events that brought together engineers and specialists from across Latin America.

<div className="row margin-bottom--lg xtreme-quote-row">
  <div className="col col--7">
    <p>Back then, the event was called Xtreme Team. I remember Martín Hoz opening one of the gatherings with these words:</p>
    <blockquote><p>“The best cybersecurity engineers from across Latin America are here today!”</p></blockquote>
  </div>
  <div className="col col--5 xtreme-quote-photo">
    <XtremeGallery photos={[{"src":"/img/xtreme-a13-martin.jpg","alt":"Martín Hoz speaking at Xtreme Team","caption":"Martín Hoz"}]} />
  </div>
</div>

Over time, the event evolved through different formats, eventually becoming today's **Fortinet Xperts Summit**.

Beyond the solutions and technologies, I remember the labs, conversations with fellow engineers, and the professional relationships and friendships built at these events.

## Events I attended

| Year | Location |
| --- | --- |
| **2007** | Mexico City, Mexico |
| **2008** | Mexico City, Mexico |
| **2009** | Mexico City, Mexico |
| **2010** | Buenos Aires, Argentina |
| **2011** | Panama City, Panama |
| **2013** | San José del Cabo, Mexico |
| **2014** | Panama City, Panama |

## Moments

Sharing sessions, knowledge and memorable moments with friends including Hernán Müller, Pablo Miño, Alexandra Castillo, Yaimarú García, Alejandro Martínez, Martín Hoz, Madwin Cerezo, Víctor Barra, David Ramírez and Héctor Salgado.

## What stays with me

Xtreme Team was part of a period of learning, exchange and community. Technologies change, but I continue to value hands-on learning and the people with whom I shared those experiences.
