# Public publication guidelines

Before publishing operational material, remove or generalize:

- Internal IP addresses, hostnames and topology details not required for the explanation.
- Credentials, tokens, API keys, cookies, certificates and private keys.
- Customer, employee or supplier information.
- Internal ticket numbers and non-public URLs.
- Screenshots containing identifiers or sensitive metadata.
- Exact defensive gaps that could materially increase exposure.

Prefer reproducible examples, sanitized configuration fragments and explicit version/context notes.
